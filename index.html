Common questions

How much does a handyman cost in Toronto?

It depends on the job. Use our estimator to get a fixed labour price for caulking, drywall and paint, trim, move-out services or flooring in under a minute.

Do you supply the materials?

Materials are not included in the labour price. You can supply them yourself, or we buy them and bill at cost with an official receipt plus a 15% procurement and handling fee. Site access and mobilization are included in your labour price.

Do you remove debris?

Yes. Choose the debris option in the estimator and hauling and disposal labour is included. Disposal facility fees, where applicable, are billed at cost. Your site is left swept and clean at handover.

What if hidden damage is found?

We tell you immediately. Any extra repair is documented in a written change order before work continues.

How do payment and cancellation work?

You can pay by Interac e-Transfer or cash. After you request a date and we confirm it, a 50% deposit by Interac e-Transfer secures your spot. If you cancel 48 hours or more before your date, part of the deposit is refunded; inside 48 hours the deposit is non-refundable.

What does painting include?

Two finish coats are included. If your colour or product needs primer or a third coat, the extra product is supplied by the client and the added labour is quoted by change order.

Which areas do you serve?

Yonge & Eglinton and surrounding Midtown Toronto neighbourhoods. Ask us if you are nearby.
