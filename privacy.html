Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

What we collect

When you request an estimate or book through this website, we collect the details you enter: your name, phone number, email address, and your preferred dates, any notes you enter, and a summary of the project and quantities you selected. If you use the website assistant, we process the questions you type and any photo you attach.

How we use it

We use your information to prepare and confirm your estimate, schedule the work, and communicate with you about your project. We do not sell your personal information.

Photos and the AI assistant

Questions and photos you submit to the website assistant are sent to a third-party artificial intelligence service so that it can generate a reply. Please do not include faces, license plates, documents, or other sensitive information in your photos. The assistant does not provide quotes; the owner confirms all details.

Service providers

We use service providers to operate this website and communicate with you, including web hosting (Netlify), form handling (Formspree), and the AI service described above. These providers process information on our behalf under their own terms and privacy policies.

Retention and your choices

We keep your information only as long as needed to respond to you, provide the services, and meet our business and legal obligations. You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us through the website request form.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising cookies. Our hosting provider may collect basic technical data, such as IP address and browser type, for security and performance.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above shows when it was last changed.
